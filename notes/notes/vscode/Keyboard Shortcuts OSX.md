### <u>Basic Editing</u>

Toggle line comment<br>
⌘ + #

Delete Line<br>
⇧⌘ + K

### <u>Multi-cursor and selection</u>

Expand selection<br>
⌃⇧ + →

Shrink selection<br>
⌃⇧ + ←

Multi Line Select<br>
⌥⌘ + ↓

### <u>Navigation</u>

Toggle between editor and terminal<br>
⌘ + ^

Go to file<br>
⌘ + P

### <u>Rich languages editing</u>

Open Chat<br>
⇧⌘ + I

Trigger suggestion<br>
⌘ + I

### <u>Display</u>

Show Explorer / Toggle focus<br>
⇧⌘E

Show Source Control<br>
⌃⇧G
